# M1a household fixture

The household in `household.json` and its ownership-and-access arrangement are a **declared test arrangement**
(Rex, 2026-11-02, SOC-R4), not a historical reconstruction of a specific medieval household.

**Limitation (PLAN H6):** Actual access to household goods after a death in this period turned on manorial custom
(widow's free bench, heriot, the lord's claims). That custom is not represented in Society; the fixture makes no
claim about real outcomes on a death beyond the declared collective arrangement.
